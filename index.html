A memory I will never forget

Before I First Talked to You ❤️

Papa, there is something I have never really told you...

Before I ever spoke to you, I was honestly scared of you. 😭😂 You looked so serious, so strict, and so intimidating that I would see you and suddenly forget all the words I wanted to say.

I remember wondering what you would think of me if I actually came up and talked to you. Would you smile? Would you be strict? Would you even want to talk to me? So instead, I stayed quiet and kept my distance, even though a part of me really wanted to know you.

But then came the day I finally found the courage to talk to you. And somehow, the person I had been so afraid of became one of the people who would mean the most to me.

With time, I realized that behind that serious face was a heart full of love, kindness, patience, and care. ❤️

Sometimes I look back and laugh at the little girl who was so afraid to say hello to you. If only she knew then what I know now... that the man she was afraid to approach would one day become someone she would love, respect, and cherish so deeply.


Now I can't imagine my life without you. ❤️
